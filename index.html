About Rimalisys

Established on 23 November 2026, RIMALISYS LTD is developing online retail operations through its own e-commerce websites and, where applicable, recognised online marketplaces. Our planned fulfilment model uses third-party suppliers to ship products directly to customers.

We are in the pre-launch stage and have not yet commenced trading. We do not currently have an active product catalogue or sales history. This corporate website provides information about our planned business and does not accept orders or payments.

Our planned business activities

Our intended product range consists of general consumer goods and accessories. Individual products and suppliers have not yet been finalised. Specific product listings, prices and delivery terms will be published when our stores launch.

01 / RETAIL

Online retail

Product selection, pricing and branding for consumer goods and accessories.

02 / SOURCING

Supplier management

Order fulfilment

Order administration and coordination of direct shipping by third-party suppliers.

Customer experience

Business development

Preparing for launch

Our next steps are to establish business banking and payment arrangements, evaluate products and suppliers, prepare our online storefronts and publish applicable customer policies before accepting orders.

01. Product preparation

Research consumer demand, evaluate products and assess potential suppliers and delivery arrangements.

02. Store preparation

03. Trading model
